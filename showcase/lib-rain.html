Backdrops, ornaments and motion. All black, white and grey, all optional, all switched on with an attribute.

Principles

  • Motion marks a state change. Rain is a still frame and moves only while work runs, through rain.busy(true), or when the reader turns it on. It respects the reader's choice and prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Theme text color only. Rain and ornaments draw in the theme's text color, white on dark and black on light. No glow.
  • Idle motion is rare and pauses. The ornament sparkle rests most of a slow cycle, pauses while the page scrolls, and stops under reduced motion. data-sd-ornaments-still turns it off for one element.
  • The browser remembers. The rain switch is remembered, and a resized sidebar keeps its width per sidebar.
  • Effects stay behind content. Inside a shell, rain skips the content, bars and dock. Elsewhere, list what covers it with data-sd-rain-skip.

Use it for

  • A shell or hero backdrop that shows work in progress.
  • Dust ornaments to give an empty state or a corner some texture.
  • Art ornaments, a drawn picture behind a card, a cover, a hero or an empty state.
  • Title bars, a compact rail and a resizable sidebar for desktop apps.

Leave it out when

  • Decorative animation. If nothing changed, nothing should move.
  • Rain behind body text. Mark the covering elements with data-sd-rain-skip.
  • Color in effects. They stay black, white and grey.

Digital rain

A still frame by default; it moves while work runs (rain.busy(true)) or when the user turns it on. Glyphs or squares, theme text color only.

<canvas class="sd-rain" data-sd-rain data-sd-rain-style="squares"> · data-sd-rain-skip=".selector" · data-sd-rain-toggle · rain.busy()
