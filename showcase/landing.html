05 Questions

Asked often.

What counts as an item?

Anything finished that needs a yes or a no: a pull request, a deploy, a report, a refund, a script run. Your tools send them; people can too.

Does my code leave my machine?

Not with the desktop app. Items and proof stay local until you approve, and only the decision is sent back to the tool.

What happens when I snooze something?

It leaves your list and comes back at the top when you said: later today, tomorrow, or Monday morning.

Can two people have to sign off?

Yes. Rules can ask for two people on anything you choose, and irreversible work always needs one.

Is there a free trial?

Team is free for 14 days with no card. Solo is free for good.
