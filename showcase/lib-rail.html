Compact rail of places

A 72px rail of groups, servers or workspaces: square tiles with a picture, initials or an icon, next to ordinary sd-rail-item tools. aria-current draws the edge on the rail's outer side; data-unread a short edge; data-pending a dashed tile for a place still joining. A corner sd-badge-count carries a count; put it in the tile's aria-label too, because the label replaces the tile's text. In the shell, set --shell-rail: 4.5rem on .sd-shell; a rail on its own takes the same variable.
