Charts

Bandwidth and latency. Live.

Time-series charts on Chart.js, with two color themes: mono, black and white with color only for thresholds, heat and alerts, and spectrum, the full signal palette. Hover for the tooltip, drag to select, use the scale modes, click the legend.

Gallery

The same tokens drive every chart type: createChart(canvas, { type }) for line, area, step, bar, ranking bars, histogram and scatter, and CSS-only squares for heatmaps, part-of-whole, uptime and meters. Series take colors 1–6 in order; in mono, lines also take a dash each.

Small plots: sd-plot

When your app already has the points and wants a small, static line, draw an SVG and let sd-plot style it. No Chart.js, no axes, no live engine. data-series picks the same color as the matching chart series, and the theme switch above applies to both.
