Effects

Backdrops, ornaments and motion. All black, white and grey, all optional, all switched on with an attribute.

Digital rain

A still frame by default; it moves while work runs (rain.busy(true)) or when the user turns it on. Glyphs or squares, theme text color only.

Ornaments and border glow

Dust in the bottom-right corner, strongest at the corner and thinning as it spreads. The border glows at the corners, fades to a hairline, and only the top edge has a bright center segment.

data-sd-ornaments + data-sd-edges

Edge dust

The same dust rising from a whole edge, softer, with a clear column at each side so dividers stay crisp. The shell uses it under the rail and the sidebar's page list.

data-sd-ornaments="bottom" or "top"

Hover shimmer

A bright band sweeps across interactive text from left to right (white on dark, black on light), once per hover. Icons, borders and backgrounds stay put. Off with reduced motion.

Compact rail of places

A 72px rail of groups, servers or workspaces: square tiles with a picture, initials or an icon, next to ordinary sd-rail-item tools. aria-current draws the edge on the rail's outer side; data-unread a short edge; data-pending a dashed tile for a place still joining. A corner sd-badge-count carries a count; put it in the tile's aria-label too, because the label replaces the tile's text. In the shell, set --shell-rail: 4.5rem on .sd-shell; a rail on its own takes the same variable.

Resizable sidebar

A role="separator" handle over the sidebar's trailing border. With data-sd-shell="resize", init() drags it, steps it with the arrow keys (16px, Shift for 64px), Home and End jump to aria-valuemin and aria-valuemax, Escape cancels a drag, and the width is remembered per sidebar id. Inside the shell it sets --shell-sidebar on .sd-shell; this page's own sidebar has one too. When a framework owns the width, leave out data-sd-shell: set --shell-sidebar and aria-valuenow yourself, and data-resizing on the shell while a drag runs.
