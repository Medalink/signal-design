Hover shimmer

A bright band sweeps across interactive text from left to right (white on dark, black on light), once per hover. Text links finish the sweep even if the pointer only brushes them. --sd-sweep-duration sets the speed and --sd-sweep-easing the curve of its progress; data-sd-sweep="progress" lets the page set --sd-sweep itself. Off with reduced motion.
