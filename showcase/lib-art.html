Mascots, pixel pieces and scenes for Signal apps. IRIS watches the network; an eagle, a squirrel and an elephant take the outdoors. All are drawn from the same squares as the ornaments, in the theme's text color.

Principles

  • Cells, not curves. Every piece is a grid of squares with crisp edges. No rounding, no anti-aliased outlines, no gradients.
  • One ink, one signal. Ink, mid and faint greys of the text color. Only the signal cells (a mascot's status light, a router's port) take a data-tone.
  • One set of moods. Every mascot shares the same eight moods and the same behavior, so swapping IRIS for the eagle changes the face, not the meaning.
  • Mascots move when something changes. A new mood, the pointer, a press. aria-busy="true" is the only loop, and a rare blink the only idle motion.
  • Whole pixels. Mascots are 15 cells; sizes and scene cells are whole multiples, so no cell lands between pixels.

Use it for

  • Empty states, onboarding and first runs.
  • A face for a system's status: calm when green, alert when red.
  • Scenes behind a welcome, a finished setup or a quiet dashboard.

Leave it out when

  • Dense data views. Art is for pauses, not for rows.
  • More than one mascot in sight outside a picker or a scene.
  • Status alone. The face sets the tone; words say what happened.

Mascots

Four characters, one behavior. Eyes turn toward the pointer, a press makes the mascot hop and smile, and aria-busy sets it working: IRIS scans, the eagle beats its wings, the squirrel swishes its tail, the elephant flaps its ears.

<button class="sd-mascot" data-sd-mascot="eagle" data-mood="idle" aria-label="Eagle"> · data-sd-mascot="iris | eagle | squirrel | elephant" · data-mood · data-tone · aria-busy="true" · setMascot(el, name) · setMood(el, mood) · sd:mascot event · sd-iris + data-sd-iris is IRIS shorthand

Moods

Eight faces for what the system is doing, shared by every mascot. Pick a mascot above and this grid follows.

data-mood="idle | calm | happy | curious | focused | sleepy | alert | offline"

Sizes

Whole multiples of the 15-cell grid, so every cell lands on whole pixels. --sd-mascot-size sets any other.

sd-mascot-sm 30px · default 60px · sd-mascot-lg 120px · sd-mascot-xl 180px

Tech pieces

Fifteen pieces for devices, data and the network, 4px per cell by default (--sd-art-cell). The signal cells take a tone.

<span class="sd-art" data-sd-art="router" role="img" aria-label="Router"> · data-flip mirrors a piece · defineArt(name, rows) adds your own

Nature pieces

Sixteen pieces for the outdoors: trees, sky, ground and water. Sun, flowers, sprouts and mushrooms carry the signal cells.

ART_SETS.tech · ART_SETS.nature list the names

Scenes

Pieces and a mascot placed on one grid. The scene picks the largest whole-pixel cell that fits its width, so it scales without blurring. Swap the mascot in every scene at once.

<div class="sd-art-scene" style="--cols: 64; --rows: 24"> with children placed by --x and --y in cells · sd-art-horizon draws the ground line

Circuit ornaments

The ornaments' tech style: traces leave the edges near a corner, turn on the grid and end in pads, thinning as they spread. The pads sparkle like the dust.

data-sd-ornaments-style="circuit" with any corner, "top" or "bottom"
