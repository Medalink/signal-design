## Overall impression

The system holds: shell and chat look and behave like real tools and color means something. The examples break on phones, skip error recovery, and break the rules they teach, which matters because they are what the owner and agents copy for Whisper, Publighter and Watcher. Biggest opportunity: make the five examples the strictest consumers of the rules.

## What's working

- Shell risk handling: #24 has pink edge, dot and RISK word, button reads Review, pressing a opens details with a toast instead of approving.
- Chat states are words plus marks (NEW divider, reply bar, mention, Not sent · Retry, poll bars, typing squares), legible in paper too.
- Mono charts: only the orange threshold and surge highlight are colored; tooltips give bucket, value and delta.

## Priority issues

[P1] Form errors hide themselves and say too little (form.html, showcase/form.js:26). firstBad.focus() scrolls the field flush to the top, hiding its label and the banner; terms checkbox red with no message; no validation before submit; banner amber while fields pink. Fix: scroll-margin-top or scrollIntoView center before focus; sd-field-error + aria-describedby on terms; validate on blur via :user-invalid; banner data-tone="danger" with a count. Command: /impeccable harden form. Evidence: form-1440-ink-errors.png.

[P1] Phones get the desktop layout (shell, chat, landing at 390). Breadcrumb collapses to "⌂ › P › A" under a duplicate label; row titles truncate; kbd hints on touch; chat stacks showcase bar, faux titlebar, ACME bar and channel header; landing hides nav with no phone menu so #pricing and #faq are unreachable. Fix: hide .sd-breadcrumb below 768px; two-line row titles; hide kbd under (pointer: coarse); titlebar sd-desktop-only; sd-phone-only menu. Command: /impeccable adapt. Evidence: shell-390-ink.png, chat-390-ink.png.

[P1] Muted states fail contrast in Signal itself (src/components/blocks.css:222 and :98). .sd-sidebar-link[data-muted] uses opacity 0.5 (2.6:1), contradicting the sd-item muted comment; .sd-item[data-muted] .sd-avatar saturate(0) leaves 3.9:1 initials. Fix: mute by color (fg-subtle text, fg-faint icon); grey avatar fill with 4.5:1 text. Command: /impeccable polish.

[P2] Color used for emphasis against the Lit Readout rule (landing, shell). Version badge data-tone="success" (landing.html:132); MOST TEAMS and Team checks green while other plans grey; approved shell rows keep an unexplained orange dot. Fix: neutral version badge; grey checks everywhere, outline marks the recommended plan; dot goes hollow/grey when done. Command: /impeccable quieter. Evidence: landing-1440-ink-pricing.png, shell-1440-ink.png.

[P2] Examples break the rules they teach (all five). landing.html: about 100 lines of .l-* CSS incl. .l-logo font-weight 600, two mask-image linear-gradient fades, box-shadow inset as a border. Shell and chat hand-copy 17 and 35 inline symbols instead of icon(). 30 inline styles in chat, 26 in charts. Decline kbd x not registered in hotkeys (shell.js:125). Charts is a catalog (API-string subtitles, gallery, formatting table). Fix: strip off-rule CSS, icon(), register x or drop it, rebuild charts as one Watcher screen and move the gallery to lib-charts; check whether signal-design check sees mask-image gradients. Command: /impeccable distill charts, then /impeccable polish.
