| `sd-voice` (+ `-play`, `-wave`, `-time`, `-speed`) | A voice note inside a message: a square filled play and pause button, a waveform, the time and a speed chip. The waveform is a `role="slider"` of `<i>` bars: `--h` (0 to 1) is a bar's peak, `--at` (0 to 1) where it sits in the note, and `--progress` (0 to 1) on the waveform lights the bars up to it, the rest staying faint. `data-playing` on `sd-voice` while it plays. The time is elapsed once the note has started, else its length. The app owns the audio element and writes the state; no JS. React: `VoiceNote` (`peaks`, `duration`, `position`, `playing`, `speed`, `onPlayingChange`, `onSeek`, `onSpeedChange`; click or drag the waveform to seek, arrows seek, Space plays; the words are props). |
