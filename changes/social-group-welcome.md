### Added
- `sd-welcome` (+ `-head`, `-name`, `-count`, `-rules`, `-labels`): the screen a new member sees once on entering a group, as a frameless block for the conversation area or a dialog body. It holds the group tile, name and member count, numbered rules, interest labels as `sd-chip` toggles (a picked one is success with a check) and a primary "Agree and enter" button.
