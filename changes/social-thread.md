### Added
- `sd-thread-chip`: the summary under a root message (up to three avatars, the reply count and the time of the last reply). A button; `aria-expanded` while its thread is open. React `ThreadChip` (`participants`, `label`, `time`, `dateTime`, `open`, `controls`).
- `sd-thread` (+ `-head`, `-title`, `-body`, `-root`, `-replies`, `-foot`): a thread panel for the shell's aside or on its own, with the root message, compact replies and a composer.
- `data-compact` on `sd-message`: no time column, for narrow panels.
