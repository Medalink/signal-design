### Added
- `sd-emoji-picker`: a group's own image emoji. A category button with `data-group` shows the group's initials or an `img`, the grid holds `img` buttons (`aria-current` marks the one the footer names), `button[data-add]` is the empty slot to add one, and the footer shows the image with its `:name:` in mono.
- `sd-emoji`: an `img` emoji sized to the line (`1.25em`) inside message text, with `alt=":name:"`.
